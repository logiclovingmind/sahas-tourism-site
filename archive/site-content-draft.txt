SAHAS means Courage & Adventure in Gujarati
Founded in Rajkot, Gujarat by two passionate adventurers — Tofik Bukhari and Milan Katariya — who have spent over 15 years exploring hidden gems across India.
SAHAS is not just a travel company. It’s a promise of safe, memorable, and soul-stirring journeys. From the coral reefs of Narara to the snow-capped peaks of Manali, every trip is personally curated by the founders.
